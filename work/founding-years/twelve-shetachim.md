# Founding years: the twelve shetachim not started before Nov 9

History session, from Nov 9 (owner: "Okay do other shetachim you haven't done yet please"): Western Pennsylvania,
Virginia (the rest), Oregon, Upstate New York, Colorado, Connecticut, Washington, Eastern Massachusetts, Eastern New
York, Maryland, Ontario, Quebec. Not Crown Heights.

Method, as for New York City (`new-york-city.md`):
- chabad.org listing dates for all 444 listings (metadata API);
- a sweep of CrownHeights.info and lubavitch.com by name and director (their WordPress search, read raw);
- web search for the main centers; Firecrawl for COLlive and Anash (`includeTags`);
- IRS ruling dates from ProPublica's API.

What stays undated gets a 'by' bound: the listing date, or an earlier IRS date of an organization with the same name
and place.

**From Virginia on: first pass only** (owner, Nov 9: get the "pass one" ones out of the way across many shetachim; don't
do the ones that take a lot of digging). Per centre: the CrownHeights.info and lubavitch.com sweep, read raw, and a
single web search for the old main centres; a year goes in only when a page states it and the page has been read raw.
Anything that needs more than that gets a listing-date bound whose note says "first pass only ... not dug into yet",
so pass two can find them. No IRS lookups in the first pass. Leads that weren't checked are in the notes as leads.

## Confidence audit (Nov 9, after the owner asked whether high and medium were being done properly)
The file's rule: high = a report from that year, or the centre itself, and nothing against it; medium = counted
back, a first mention, one secondary source, or sources a year apart; low = one weak source. Of the 97 years the first
pass added from Virginia on, 70 had been rated high, and 31 of those rested on one later article, an anniversary count,
or sources a year apart. They are now medium (and Mada low), each note saying why. Kept high: a report from that
year, the centre's own page or the shliach's own words, two sources that agree, or one source plus a chabad.org
listing made that same year (after 2004). Newton and Camp Emunah kept high with a second source found in the sweep.
Now: 39 high, 57 medium, 1 low. Across the twelve: 54 of 449 listings high (12%; 46% of the 118 with a year).
Every medium still needs a second, independent source before a shetach is called done (HANDOFF rule); that is pass
two. `progress.py` now prints the HIGH share first (the owner's metric, Nov 8).
