# Founding years: Central Massachusetts, New Haven, Virginia, Oregon, Upstate New York

Third session, from Nov 8 (owner: "Get started on Central Massachusetts, New Haven, Virginia, Oregon, and upstate New
York."). Method as in `wisconsin-illinois-ohio.md`: each listing searched by name (COLlive, CrownHeights.info and
lubavitch.com through their WordPress search, plus local papers that answer plain requests); sources read raw (curl)
before a year rests on them, never WebFetch; chabad.org listing dates (`api/content/<id>/metadata?tid=7152`) and
lubavitch.com addresses for every listing. Goal: high confidence (owner, Nov 8), so mediums and ranges say why they
are not high. Gannett papers (the Worcester Telegram, MetroWest Daily News, Wicked Local) refuse plain requests (402),
so their stories are read only where CrownHeights.info or COLlive reprinted them.

## Central Massachusetts (batch central-massachusetts-01): 14 listings, 7 with a year, 3 high
- Worcester (the regional office at 22 Newton Avenue, the yeshiva's address): 1942, medium. Rabbi Hershel Fogelman's
  COLlive obituary (2013) and the Worcester Telegram (2007) say 1942, and Rebbetzin Rivkah Hecht's obituary (2010)
  says the Frierdiker Rebbe sent the Hechts to Worcester in 1942. But Lubavitch.com's obituary of Rabbi Fogelman has
  him in Buffalo from 1943 and in Worcester from 1946, and his wife's (2025) says they married in 1947 and went
  straight to Worcester. The year is the start of the Worcester yeshiva; the Fogelmans' own arrival is 1946-47.
- Framingham 1978 (high, Rabbi Lazaros's obituary, 2026). Sudbury Jan 2006 (high, the Sudbury Town Crier). Mansfield
  Dec 2005 (high, the Sun Chronicle).
- Mediums are count-backs: Natick about 1987 (its 26th gala in Dec 2013), Wellesley-Weston about 1999 ("18 years
  ago" in Aug 2017), Shrewsbury about 2017 ("more than three years ago" in July 2021, listed Nov 2017).
- Ranges (7): the Jewish Women's Circle (by 2014), Chabad of the Boroughs (by 2022), Acton-Concord and Milford (by
  2004, the first online directory), the Worcester mikvah (by 2004), Bais Chana girls' high school (after 1947, by
  2004), Worcester Chabad on Campus (by 2012).

## New Haven (batch new-haven-01): 15 listings plus Oxford, 6 with a year, 5 high
- Southern Connecticut Hebrew Academy: 1945, medium (the Chinuch Office directory: "Elementary Founded: 1945"; COLlive
  2026 "nearly 80 years"; the New Haven Register "in the 1940s"). Not high: Rebbetzin Hecht's obituary has the
  Hechts arriving in New Haven in 1946.
- Yale 2002 (high, the Jewish Ledger, 2011). The high school 1976 (high, the New Haven Independent quoting the
  yeshiva: founded "in the home of Reb Dovid & Sara Deitsch"). Wallingford Nov 2005 (high, the Record-Journal).
  Milford spring 2007 (high: the New Shluchim notice of July 2007, and the New York Times, Dec 2007, on the Hebrew
  Congregation of Woodmont, a 1926 shul that hired Rabbi Schneur Wilhelm "last spring"; the year is when Chabad came,
  as with other older shuls a shliach took on). Oxford summer 2023 (high, COLlive; not on chabad.org's directory, an
  extra center on the map).
- Ranges (10): Camp Gan Israel, the Rabbinical Institute, Hamden, Chabad of the Shoreline (Guilford; its festival
  began in 2006) and the mikvah (all by 2004), Colel Tiferes Zekainim (by 2015), Westville (by 2006), CTeen (by
  2021), Downtown New Haven (by 2022; a Chabad menorah lighting at Union Station in Dec 2021), Young Anash (by 2024).
