## A new tool: chabad.org's `mosad-id`
`api/v2/chabadorg/centers/<id>?format=jsonapi` has a second number, `mosad-id`, the old (pre-2004) directory's own
id. For listings made after 2004 it rises with the listing date. For the first-day (July 22 2004) listings:
ids up to about 1,400 are the original list (alphabetical by state, then departments); ids 3,250-4,030 were
given in order as entries were added in about 2001 to mid-2004 (median founding year of the dated ones:
3250-3500 → 2001, 3500-3750 → 2002, 3750-4000 → 2003, 4000+ → 2004). So for the open ones it says when the
directory entry was made (a lead, like a listing date; old centers were also entered late):
- about 2001: Chabad of Bay Ridge (3382), Chabad VSL (3375, but there by 1995), Chabad Lubavitch Education W. St. Paul (3387),
  Montreal's Chai Friendship Club / Torah Fax / Beth Hamedrash Lubavitch (3409-3418)

  Kolel Tiferet Zkanim LA (3525), West Coast Rabbinical Seminary (3594)
- about 2002-03: Mayan Yisroel (3660); about 2003: Chabad of Patchogue (3746)
- first half of 2004: The Living Legacy Paramus (3811), Chabad of Ridgefield (3813), Chabad CJL Hollywood /
  Emerald Hills (3816), Lubavitch of NW Philadelphia (3818)

## Tools that worked
- Firecrawl reads the Wayback Machine (`web.archive.org/web/<ts>id_/<url>`, 1 credit; `formats: ["query"]` gives
  a short answer for 5). The container reaches `http://archive.org/wayback/available?url=..&timestamp=..` free
  (closest capture of one URL). WebFetch cannot read web.archive.org.
- IRS files by state: `https://www.irs.gov/pub/irs-soi/eo_<st>.csv` (name and address search, free).
- Not yet tried, per listing: regional paper archives with full text (Pittsburgh Jewish Chronicle at CMU,
  American Jewish World at MNHS, Jewish Transcript Seattle, Jewish Review on Historic Oregon Newspapers,
  Canadian Jewish News at SFU, Ottawa Jewish Bulletin, Jewish Floridian at UF, Washington Post archive for Potomac).
Firecrawl this session: roughly 70 credits of searches and page reads, plus six research-agent runs (their cost
is not shown here).
